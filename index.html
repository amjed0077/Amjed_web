WHO IS AMJED?

Turning ideas into experiences people can feel.

Based in Djerba, Amjed Ben Chouikha works at the intersection of tourism, digital marketing and creative communication. His work moves from strategy to execution: shaping offers, building brand stories, creating content and bringing campaigns to life.

With experience around travel agencies, hospitality, destinations and events, he approaches each project from both sides — the business objective and the audience experience.
